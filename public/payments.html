Get paid with a QR code

Connect your wallet to create a QR code. The payer scans it with their phone camera and this page opens with your address filled in.

How it works

  1. Hedgora checks the address, amount and network fee, and tells you whether you have paid this address before.
  2. You review the total, fee included, before anything is sent.
  3. Circle’s secure window opens. USDC only leaves your wallet once you confirm there.
